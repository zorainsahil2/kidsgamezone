Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026
🛡️ COPPA COMPLIANT
This portal does not collect, request, or share any personal information from children.

Welcome to KidsGameZone! We are dedicated to providing a safe, secure, and fun environment for children to play educational and arcade browser games. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the collection, use, and security of information on our portal.

1. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting bacchon ki privacy is our absolute priority. We comply fully with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)**.

  • No Personal Information: We do **NOT** request, collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13.
  • No Accounts Required: Children do not need to register, create accounts, or enter usernames, emails, or phone numbers to play any games on this portal.

2. Information We Log & How We Protect It

To keep the website stable, monitor game popularity, and track server issues, we automatically log anonymous analytical metrics.

  • Hashed IP Addresses: We do **NOT** save raw IP addresses. When a user loads a game, their IP address is instantly hashed using the SHA-256 algorithm (`ip_hash`) before database storage to ensure absolute anonymity.
  • Anonymized Metrics: We track counts of game views, ad impressions, and ad skip events. No visitor profile is ever created.

3. Ad Networks and Cookies

To fund the hosting of our 50 free games, we display family-friendly advertisements.

  • Contextual Advertising Only: Our selected ad networks (e.g. Google AdSense, Media.net, Adsterra) are restricted to showing kid-safe, contextual, and non-behavioral advertisements. We do not support retargeting or personalized ads tracking children's web habits.
  • Cookies Usage: Cookies are used strictly for service performance, rate limiting (preventing tracking spam), and saving game scores locally in your browser.

4. Parental Rights & Controls

Parents are in complete control of their children's web experience. Under COPPA, parents have the right to review what anonymous metrics are logged or request total erasure.

If you have any questions, concerns, or inquiries, please contact our administration immediately via email at: support@kidsgamezone.com.

5. Policy Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect modifications in regulations or website features. We advise parents to check this page occasionally to stay informed.
